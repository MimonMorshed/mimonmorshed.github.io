Mimon Morsheduzzaman

PhD student studying Gulf of Mexico shelf circulation and its response to atmospheric forcing.

I'm a PhD Student in the Department of Oceanography and Coastal Sciences at Louisiana State University, working in the WAVCIS Lab under Dr. Chunyan Li. My research centers on how Gulf of Mexico shelf circulation responds to atmospheric forcing, using High Frequency Radar observations to see currents at a resolution most models can't reach.
